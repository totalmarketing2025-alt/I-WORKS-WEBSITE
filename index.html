01 / About

Technology driven by curiosity. Built for impact.

I-WORKS is an independent collective of developers, engineers and technology enthusiasts based in North Macedonia.

Our community brings together diverse technical perspectives to explore and develop software for digital security, private communications, intelligent monitoring and humanitarian technology.

We believe meaningful innovation begins with independent thinking, collaboration and responsible engineering.
