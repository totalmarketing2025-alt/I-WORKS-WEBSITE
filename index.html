01 / STELLARECC

StellarECC

PRIVATE COMMUNICATIONS

What is StellarECC?

A secure communication application designed to make private digital conversations more resilient and privacy-focused.

How does it work?

Users communicate through the app, while its security architecture is designed to protect message content and support secure exchange. Specific protections and supported features depend on the released build and configuration.

Why are we building it?

To give people and organizations a communications experience built around privacy, control and dependable engineering.

02 / SARISA

SARISA

AI-ASSISTED SEARCH & RESCUE

What is SARISA?

System for Advanced Real-time Intelligent Search and Action: a developing software platform for drone-assisted missions, navigation and situational awareness.

How does it work?

The concept combines an operator dashboard, drone video, mission planning and computer-vision assistance. The operator reviews observations and remains responsible for operational decisions. Real-world capabilities require integration and validation.

Why are we building it?

To explore ways intelligent tools can help teams search difficult areas, organize information and support rescue operations.

03 / HAVOC GUARD

HAVOC GUARD

INTELLIGENT VIDEO SECURITY

What is HAVOC GUARD?

A CCTV and IP-camera monitoring concept that uses video analytics to support authorized security teams in understanding activity within camera-covered facilities and perimeters.

How does it work?

Camera feeds can be analyzed for visible people, movement, interactions and predefined security events. An operator can review alerts and last-observed locations within the monitored area. Identification, when enabled, requires appropriate authorization and safeguards.

What is its purpose?

To help authorized operators detect incidents faster, review evidence and improve situational awareness—not to automatically determine criminal intent, guilt or private relationships.

Capabilities subject to implementation and validation
