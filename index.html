SARISA

System for Advanced Real-time Intelligent Search and Action — an operator-supervised search-and-rescue and situational-awareness platform for Android and Windows.

ANDROID BUILD ✓WINDOWS BUILD ✓
  • Offline YOLOX-Nano analysis of Android camera frames and selected Windows images.
  • SAR target appearance search with up to eight reference photos; operator-reviewed and session-only, with no identity recognition.

03 / WILDFIRE EARLY WARNING CONCEPT · PILOT DESIGN

FIRE EYE

Fire Eye is a proposed wildfire early-warning network for forests and green spaces, combining panoramic camera coverage, local AI analysis and satellite backhaul to help operators identify possible fire signals earlier.

  • Four overlapping wide-angle camera sectors provide continuous panoramic watch; a separate optical PTZ unit can zoom in to verify a possible signal.
  • On-site AI flags possible smoke or flame, stores the full feed locally, and sends an alert image and short clip to the operations center.
  • Starlink is planned for remote-site connectivity. Camera direction and intersecting views help estimate the fire area on the central map.

Concept / pilot design. Proposed ~50 m towers and up-to-20 km² site coverage are preliminary targets. Camera zoom, visibility, AI accuracy and connectivity need vendor and terrain testing. No live towers or emergency-service integration are active.

04 / INTELLIGENT VIDEO SECURITY CONCEPT PREVIEW

HAVOC GUARD

An intelligent CCTV and IP-camera monitoring concept for authorized facilities and perimeters. The system is intended to help operators review camera-covered movement, predefined security events and last-observed locations. AI flags require human verification.

  • Multi-camera monitoring and event timelines
  • Visible person, vehicle and restricted-zone event detection
  • Human-reviewed alerts, privacy and access controls
